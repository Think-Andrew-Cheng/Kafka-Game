# In the Penal Colony: The Apparatus

A first-person literary game based on Franz Kafka’s *In the Penal Colony*.

## Play on Mac

**[Download the Mac game](https://github.com/Think-Andrew-Cheng/Kafka-Game/releases/download/v1.0.2-mac-playtest/In-the-Penal-Colony-Mac.zip)** · [Release notes](https://github.com/Think-Andrew-Cheng/Kafka-Game/releases/tag/v1.0.2-mac-playtest)

1. Download and extract the ZIP.
2. Open the **In the Penal Colony** folder.
3. Open **Apparatus.app**. You do not need Godot or Unreal Engine.

The download supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, with macOS 12 or later. This is an ongoing playtest build, ad-hoc signed and not Apple-notarized. See **READ ME FIRST.txt** inside the ZIP for first-launch instructions, controls, and requirements. Intel hardware has not been playtested.

## Controls

- **WASD / arrows:** walk; **mouse:** look.
- **E / click:** inspect and interact.
- **Space / Enter:** continue dialogue; **1–3:** choose a reply.
- **Escape:** menu and settings.
- **Strap minigame:** walk around the bed with WASD / arrows; hold right mouse to look; use left mouse to handle straps.

Keep the game open to preserve your current playthrough; this build does not have chapter saves. Use Low quality in Settings if performance is uneven.
